# Privacy Policy for LeetLens

*Last Updated: Today*

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the LeetLens browser extension ("the Extension").

## 1. Information Collection and Use
LeetLens is designed with a strict "Privacy First" architecture. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal information, code, or analytics to our servers.**

All data generated by the Extension—including your Learning Profile, Weekly Goals, past Reviews, Visualizations, and Concept Cards—is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

## 2. API Keys and Third-Party AI Providers
To function, LeetLens requires you to provide an API key for a third-party AI provider (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, or OpenRouter).
- Your API key is stored locally on your device.
- We never transmit your API key to any servers other than the direct API endpoint of the provider you selected.
- When you request an AI feature (e.g., a Code Review), the Extension sends your active LeetCode code snippet directly to your selected AI provider. Please refer to the privacy policies of your selected provider (e.g., Google or OpenAI) to understand how they handle data submitted via their API.

## 3. Telemetry and Analytics
LeetLens does not include any external analytics, trackers, or telemetry. We have no mechanism to track your usage, what problems you solve, or how often you use the Extension.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
