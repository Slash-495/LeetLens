# LeetLens

**Your Personal AI Coding Mentor for LeetCode**

LeetLens is a powerful, privacy-first Chrome Extension that transforms your LeetCode experience. Instead of just giving you the answers, LeetLens acts as a Senior Staff Engineer sitting right beside you, helping you build genuine intuition for data structures and algorithms.

## Features

🧠 **Conceptual Code Reviews**
Write your code and hit "Review". LeetLens will analyze your exact approach, estimate Time and Space Complexity, catch hidden edge cases, and evaluate your interview readiness—just like a real mock interview.

👁️ **Visual Execution Engine**
Struggling to trace pointers? LeetLens generates step-by-step visual execution traces of your code running on sample inputs. Watch your variables change and arrays mutate in real-time.

⚖️ **Tradeoff & Comparison Engine**
Compare your brute-force solution side-by-side with the optimal approach. LeetLens explains the tradeoffs, the architecture, and even renders a dual visualizer so you can watch both algorithms race!

🎓 **Smart Learning Paths**
LeetLens locally analyzes your strengths and weaknesses to recommend exactly what problems you should solve next. It can even generate "Easier" and "Harder" variants of your current problem.

👶 **"Explain Like I'm 12" Mode**
Every piece of advice in LeetLens features an ELI12 button. Click it to instantly translate dense engineering jargon into fun, easy-to-understand real-world analogies.

## Privacy First (Bring Your Own Key)

We believe your code and your data belong to you. LeetLens operates entirely locally:
- **No telemetry.**
- **No external databases.**
- **No account required.**

You supply your own API key (Gemini, OpenAI, or OpenRouter), and LeetLens communicates *directly* from your browser to the provider. Your API key is stored securely in your browser's local storage and never touches our servers.
